Projects

Event-Guided Diffusion Transformer for Video Frame Interpolation Oct. 2025 – Jul. 2026
Supervised by Prof. Yongjian Deng at Data Mining and Security Lab.
Read more »

I developed an Event-Guided Diffusion Transformer for video frame interpolation, integrating asynchronous event streams into a DiT backbone via cross-attention to resolve severe motion blur. Additionally, I designed a motion-aware loss reweighting strategy with representation alignment, reducing LPIPS error by 21.4% and improving FloLPIPS consistency by 27.3% over SOTA baselines on Middlebury and SNU-FILM.

Unified Vision-Language Model for Photovoltaic Remote Sensing Jul. 2025 – May 2026
Supervised by Prof. Zhiling Guo and Prof. Jinyue Yan at DigiEnergy Lab.
Read more »

This project developed a PV-specific vision-language model based on a curated 1M-pair dataset to enable zero-shot and few-shot learning for downstream photovoltaic interpretation tasks. I designed the Knowledge Assistance Module via gated-convolution to inject spatial priors for downstream few-shot adaptation, reaching 97.96% classification accuracy, 51.51% localization mSC, and +18.40% IoU gain in zero-shot segmentation, alongside a 19.35% IoU gain in 5-sample segmentation and a 16.7% AP@0.5 boost in detection.

Generative Foreign Object Detection in High-Speed Railway Apr. 2024 – Dec. 2025
Supervised by Prof. Liguo Zhang at Cyber-Physical Network System Lab.
Read more »

This project developed a generative foreign object detection system coupling diffusion-based sample synthesis with SA-YOLO object detection to address severe data scarcity in high-speed railway environments. I developed a generation-extraction-fusion pipeline integrating a LoRA-fine-tuned Stable Diffusion model. This pipeline achieves batch synthesis of high-fidelity intrusion foreign object samples under complex lighting and dynamic high-speed rail environments, effectively enriching training distributions and overcoming annotation bottlenecks.

Synthetic-Driven Multimodal AI for PV Segmentation Feb. 2024 – Nov. 2025
Supervised by Prof. Zhiling Guo at DigiEnergy Lab.
Read more »

This project developed a generative data synthesis and multimodal segmentation framework to tackle label scarcity and environmental interferences in urban photovoltaic remote sensing. Specifically, I built a latent diffusion framework to synthesize paired urban remote sensing images and pixel-aligned masks, where joint training with only 10 real samples increased segmentation IoU across three city test sets by 9.60%, 9.13%, and 20.55%. Additionally, I designed a physical-guided SAM decoder to eliminate shadow and reflection interference, achieving 0.9642 Precision and 0.9262 IoU.

Diffusion-Based Meteorological Super-Resolution for PV Estimation Oct. 2023 – Sept. 2024
Supervised by Prof. Zhiling Guo at DigiEnergy Lab.
Read more »

I developed a score-based diffusion super-resolution method for multimodal meteorological data, incorporating a SwinT-DyS module that fuses shifted-window self-attention with dynamic grid upsampling to capture fine-grained spatial microclimate patterns. This method surpassed SOTA baselines across solar irradiance metrics with a 10.26% MAE reduction, 1.37% PSNR gain, and 7.41% SSIM boost, leveraging the super-resolved fields to enable 2km-resolution rooftop PV potential mapping.

3D Human Pose Reconstruction System Sept. 2023 – May 2024
Supervised by Prof. Liguo Zhang at Cyber-Physical Network System Lab.
Read more »

I designed an edge 3D reconstruction system integrating real-time YOLOv8 pose estimation with physical geometric priors, leveraging dynamic spatial filtering to eliminate self-occlusion distortion and background noise in complex environments.
